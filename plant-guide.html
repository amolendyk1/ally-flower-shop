Plants

Your guide to selecting and caring for beautiful plants — from indoor greenery to outdoor garden favorites.

Snake Plant

Climate: Arid / tropical. Extremely adaptable indoors.

Sun: Low to bright indirect light — very forgiving.

Water: Very low — water every 3–4 weeks.

Soil: Well‑draining cactus mix.

Care tips: Nearly indestructible. Excellent air purifier.

Striped Calathea

Climate: Tropical / humid.

Sun: Low to bright indirect light.

Water: Keep soil evenly moist.

Soil: Peat‑based mix with perlite.

Care tips: Mist daily. Avoid cold drafts.

Anthurium

Climate: Tropical / humid.

Sun: Bright indirect light.

Water: Keep soil moist. Mist regularly.

Soil: Peat‑based mix with perlite.

Care tips: Fertilize monthly. Repot every 2 years.
